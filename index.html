Questions

Before you buy

Who is this for?

First-time and cautious used car buyers, whether you are buying from a dealership or a private seller.

Does it cover my state?

It covers the federal rules and the steps that apply in every state, and flags what changes by state: taxes, fees, title transfer, inspections and cancellation rights. State rules differ, so the kit links to the official sources to confirm yours.

Do I need to know about cars?

No. Everything is written in plain English and explains what to look for, why it matters and what to do about it.

Can I use it with a dealership?

Yes. It has a dealership question list, financing tools and guidance on warranties, add-ons and return policies.

Can I use it with a private seller?

Yes. It covers ownership paperwork, outstanding finance and liens, and warning signs of scams.

Does it replace a mechanic's inspection?

No. It helps you spot warning signs and decide when to get an independent inspection, which is recommended for most purchases.

Is the financing calculator a loan approval tool?

No. It estimates payments and total cost from the numbers you enter. A lender decides approval, and your signed agreement sets the real terms.
